Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DECTO ("we", "us") collects through this website and how it is used. DECTO is an early-stage project; this policy will be expanded as the product develops.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

  • Email address — if you submit the waitlist form, your email is used solely to contact you about DECTO updates and product launches.
  • No automatic tracking — this site does not currently use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking scripts.

2. Third-Party Data

This site displays live cryptocurrency market data fetched directly from the CoinGecko public API. This is a one-way data request made by your browser; no personal or identifying information is sent to CoinGecko as part of that request.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any email address submitted through the waitlist form is used only to send you updates about DECTO. We do not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain waitlist email addresses only for as long as needed to keep you informed about DECTO's launch. You can request removal at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you've provided by contacting us at the email below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as DECTO evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to dectoai@gmail.com.

This is a starter privacy policy template and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before DECTO handles real user data at scale (especially given its financial/trading focus), we recommend having this reviewed by legal counsel to ensure compliance with applicable regulations (e.g. GDPR, CCPA).
